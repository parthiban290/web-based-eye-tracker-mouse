✦ Features

Everything You Need for
Hands-Free Control

EyeFlow combines advanced computer vision with intuitive design to give you complete control of your computer using just your eyes.

👁

Eye Tracking Cursor

Your cursor follows your gaze in real-time. Using MediaPipe's 468-point face mesh and iris detection, we track where you're looking and move the cursor smoothly across the screen with head-compensation technology.

🟠

Blink to Click

Simply blink your right eye to perform a mouse click. Our advanced Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) algorithm detects intentional blinks with 95%+ accuracy, with built-in debounce to prevent accidental clicks.

✏️

Voice Typing

Click on any text field and start speaking. EyeFlow automatically activates voice recognition when you focus on a text input, converting your speech to text in real-time. Supports multiple languages.

📜

Nose Scroll

Tilt your head slightly up or down to scroll through pages. Our nose-tracking algorithm uses a comfortable dead-zone to prevent accidental scrolling, giving you smooth and precise scroll control.

🔒

Privacy First

All processing happens locally on your machine. No video data is ever stored or sent to external servers. Your webcam feed is processed in real-time and immediately discarded. Your privacy is our priority.

⚡

Low Latency

Experience sub-100ms response times for smooth, natural cursor control. Our optimized pipeline processes 30 frames per second with exponential moving average smoothing for jitter-free movement.

⚙️ How it Works

Simple Setup,
Powerful Control

Get started in minutes with just a webcam. No special hardware needed.

1

Create Your Account

Sign up with a username and password. Your calibration settings and preferences are saved securely to your profile so you can pick up right where you left off every time.

2

Calibrate Your Eyes

Look at 5 different points on your screen and press SPACE for each one. This teaches EyeFlow the range of your eye movements. Calibration takes just 30 seconds and is saved to your profile.

3

Launch the Tracker

Click "Start Now" from the dashboard. EyeFlow opens your webcam, loads your calibration, and begins tracking. You'll see live stats including FPS, accuracy, and latency.

4

Control Your Computer

Switch to any application — your cursor follows your gaze. Blink to click, tilt your head to scroll, and click on text fields to activate voice typing. EyeFlow works across your entire system.

5

Fine-Tune Settings

Adjust cursor smoothing, blink sensitivity, scroll speed, and more from the dashboard. Enable or disable voice typing, choose your language, and toggle sound feedback — all in real-time.

♿ Accessibility

Built for
Everyone

EyeFlow is designed to empower individuals with motor impairments and physical disabilities to use computers independently.

🌍

1 Billion+ People Impacted

Over 1 billion people worldwide have disabilities affecting mobility. Traditional mouse and keyboard input is inaccessible to many. EyeFlow provides an affordable alternative that works with just a webcam.

💰

95% Cost Reduction

Commercial eye tracking systems cost $5,000–$15,000. EyeFlow is completely free and open source, requiring only a standard webcam that most laptops already have built-in.

🖥️

Cross-Platform

Works on Windows, macOS, and Linux. No special software installation required beyond Python. Access through any modern web browser for the dashboard and control panel.

📋

WCAG 2.1 Compliant

Our interface follows Web Content Accessibility Guidelines with high contrast dark theme, clear typography, keyboard navigation support, and screen reader compatible elements.

🗣️

Multi-Language Voice

Voice typing supports English, Tamil, Hindi, Spanish, French, and more. Users can type in their native language by simply speaking, removing the barrier of keyboard-based input.

🎯

Personalized Calibration

Each user's calibration is saved individually. The system adapts to different eye shapes, face structures, and seating positions. Re-calibrate anytime for optimal accuracy.
